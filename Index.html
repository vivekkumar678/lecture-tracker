Welcome to Lecture Tracker

A study planner for your lectures. Plan them, study them, then revise them before you forget.

How it works

  1. Add a subjectTap New Subject, name it and say how many lectures it has. Add an exam date and the app tells you how many lectures a day you need.
  2. Set target datesPick a date on the first lecture, then choose how many lectures per day (1, 2, 3… up to 20) and how often to study. Or tap Smart Plan to auto-schedule everything from your exam date.
  3. StudyPress the ▶ button on a lecture to start a focus timer. Time is saved against that lecture. Add notes, a priority or a star to anything that matters.
  4. Tick it offCheck the box when you finish. Finished lectures count towards your progress, streak, XP level and daily goal.
  5. ReviseFinished lectures come back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Tap Revise now when it appears.

Where to look

  • Dashboard — your greeting, level, stats, up-next lecture and what needs attention.
  • Subjects — every subject and lecture with search, filters and sorting.
  • Agenda — grouped lists of overdue, due today, due soon, revisions and exams.
  • Insights — streak, activity map, study time and achievements.
  • Settings — daily goal, timers, accent colour and sharing.
  • Command palette — press Ctrl + K to jump anywhere.

Your data

  • Everything saves in this browser automatically. Sign in from the sidebar to sync across devices.
  • Install as an app by choosing Add to Home Screen from your browser menu.
  • The two sample subjects can be deleted with the bin icon whenever you like.

Smart Plan

Give your exam date and rate each subject. The plan dates every pending lecture, gives weak subjects more urgency, and keeps days free for revision.

Schedule dates

Lectures are spaced evenly from the start date to this date.
